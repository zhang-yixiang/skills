## What it does

Inspect a development session for recurring failures or costly friction and propose the smallest durable environment improvement.

## When to reach for it

Invoke explicitly after a relevant incident or recurring problem. It is optional, not a compulsory final delivery step.

## Common questions

**Does every problem become another rule?**
No. Repair an existing check or remove conflicting guidance first. Mechanical patterns may need deterministic checks; judgement calls may need concise guidance. No durable change is a valid outcome.

**Does a missing CI job require new infrastructure?**
No. Judge the actual local workflow and failure evidence. Do not prescribe a hook or CI merely because it is absent.

**Can it modify configuration automatically?**
Only within an improvement already authorized by the user. Otherwise it proposes, with evidence, expected benefit, and maintenance cost.

## It's working if

Changes address observed causes, remove unnecessary burden where possible, and do not turn one session preference into a permanent universal rule.
